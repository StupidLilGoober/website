<h2>Watching Godzilla: Minus Zero With My Family</h2>
<p>
  Recently, I went to the theaters to watch Godzilla: Minus Zero with my family. You
  may not know this, but my family are <strong>pretty big</strong> Godzilla fans, and that is
  the grossest understatement I have ever made. As we speak, my grandma on my step-mom's
  side is trying to collect all of the Godzilla movies in the mainline series.
</p>
<p>
  Anyway, back on point, this is probably just recency bias but I think that this movie
  was just as good as Minus One. If you've seen Minus One, you'd know that is extremely
  high praise.
</p>
<p>
  I also think that it had one of the most brutal fight scenes in the entire franchise,
  to the point it almost made me uncomfortable (not necessarily a complaint).
</p>
<p>
  Just like the original Minus One, it made me tear up. I don't want to say what happens
  because some people probably haven't seen it yet, but it's just so good at bringing out
  emotion. <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takashi_Yamazaki">Takashi Yamazaki</a>
  is turning out to be a creative genius.
</p>
